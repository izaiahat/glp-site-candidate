First-party internal application

The campaign workspace behind GLP Clinic Scout.

GLP Clinic Scout uses this application to let a small set of authorized Tilton Group LLC operators plan, manage, and measure our own Google Ads campaigns. It is not a public clinic service, advertising platform, or customer-account connector.

Authorized operators onlyNo public sign-upOur advertising accounts only
Purpose

One controlled system for our own campaigns.

The workspace uses the Google Ads API to replace manual campaign administration with a documented, permissioned operating flow. Access is limited to approved Tilton Group LLC operators.

01 · PLAN

Campaign planning

Research keyword ideas and forecasts, organize campaign structure, and prepare account-specific recommendations before changes are made.

02 · MANAGE

Campaign management

Create and update our campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, budgets, bids, targeting, and status through controlled internal workflows.

03 · MEASURE

Reporting and review

Read performance metrics and Google Ads recommendations so authorized operators can monitor results and make accountable decisions.

Authorization

Deliberately narrow by design.

The application requests only the Google Ads permission required for its stated campaign functions. It does not ask for Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or unrelated Google Account data.

1

An approved operator authorizes access.
The Google consent screen identifies GLP Clinic Scout and the requested Google Ads permission.

2

Access is used for first-party work.
The app operates only on advertising accounts Tilton Group LLC is authorized to manage.

3

Control remains with the operator.
Authorization can be revoked through the Google Account permissions page or by contacting us.

Data boundary

Clear use. Clear exclusions.

Google Ads data is used only to operate, secure, and improve this internal campaign workflow. It is not sold or used to build advertising products for outside customers.

Data we use

  • Campaign, ad group, ad, keyword, budget, bid, targeting, and status data
  • Performance reports, recommendations, and keyword-planning output
  • OAuth authorization and security metadata

What we do not provide

  • No public user accounts or outside advertiser connections
  • No consumer medical care, diagnosis, or treatment
  • No sale of Google user data or generalized AI-model training

Your controls

  • Review the app-specific privacy policy
  • Revoke Google Account authorization at any time
  • Request access deletion or support from our monitored contact

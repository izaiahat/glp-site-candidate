Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026. This policy applies to the GLP Clinic Scout campaign operations application at glpclinicscout.com/google-ads-api/. It supplements, and is separate from, the consumer-site privacy policy.

Google API Limited Use disclosure. GLP Clinic Scout's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Who operates this application

Tilton Group LLC operates GLP Clinic Scout and this first-party internal application. Access is limited to authorized Tilton Group LLC operators. The application is not offered as a public SaaS product and does not connect arbitrary outside advertisers.

2. Google data the application accesses

The application requests the Google Ads authorization scope. Depending on the functions an authorized operator uses, it may access:

  • Google Ads customer-account identifiers and account configuration;
  • campaign, ad group, ad, keyword, budget, bid, targeting, status, asset, and recommendation data;
  • performance metrics, reports, keyword ideas, and forecasts;
  • OAuth authorization, token, access, security, and audit metadata.

The application does not request Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or unrelated Google Account data.

3. How Google data is used

We use Google Ads data only to provide and improve the application's disclosed first-party functions: campaign planning, campaign and advertising-account management, reporting, optimization review, reliability, auditability, and security. We do not use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Storage, protection, and retention

OAuth credentials and tokens are held in restricted-access server-side secret storage and are not placed in public source code or public documents. Operational records, campaign configuration, performance snapshots, and audit logs may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and document the application or meet legal obligations. Access is limited to authorized personnel and service providers with an operational need.

5. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell Google user data. We do not transfer it to data brokers, use it for generalized AI-model training, or use it to serve advertising for third parties. Data may be available only to authorized Tilton Group LLC operators and service providers acting solely to host, operate, secure, or troubleshoot the application; to comply with law; or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable protections.

6. Visitor data, cookies, and tracking

This informational microsite has no account registration, forms, analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, or third-party scripts. Standard hosting and security infrastructure may process limited request information such as IP address, user agent, timestamp, and requested URL for delivery, abuse prevention, and security logging.

7. Authorization, revocation, and deletion

An authorized user can revoke access through Google Account permissions. To request support, deletion of stored Google authorization data, or information about retained operational records, email privacy@glpclinicscout.com. Revocation may stop future access but does not automatically delete records we must retain for security, legal, or audit purposes.

8. Consumer and health information

This application is for advertising operations. It does not provide clinical care and is not intended to process protected health information, treatment records, diagnosis information, or consumer medical inquiries.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when the application's Google-data practices materially change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

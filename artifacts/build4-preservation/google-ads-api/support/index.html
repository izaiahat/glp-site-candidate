This support page is for the internal GLP Clinic Scout campaign operations application. Consumer comparison questions should use the support path on glpclinicscout.com.

Monitored support contact

Email support@glpclinicscout.com for OAuth authorization, access revocation, Google-data deletion, security, or application-function questions.

Before contacting us

  • Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, developer tokens, bank details, patient information, or government identifiers by email.
  • For access revocation, you may first remove GLP Clinic Scout from Google Account permissions.
  • Include a concise description of the issue and whether you are an authorized Tilton Group LLC operator.

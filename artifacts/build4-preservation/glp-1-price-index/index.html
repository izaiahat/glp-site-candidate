Methodology

What this index counts—and what it refuses to guess

Observation date
2026-09-03
Provider universe
59 live, wired, unique providers after declared merchant aliases are removed.
First-party sources
35 product observations, each linked to the provider page captured in Clinic Scout’s price lineage.
Honest history
History begins 2026-08-08 and contains 12 observed weekly snapshots. No earlier weeks were backfilled.

Statistic

For each medication, use one eligible lowest explicit monthly or provider-stated monthly-equivalent amount per live unique provider, sort the amounts, and report the middle value as the median plus the observed minimum, maximum, and spread.

Inclusion rule

Include a product-specific compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide amount only when the current Clinic Scout content ledger preserves a dated HTTPS first-party source, exact captured wording, an explicit monthly or monthly-equivalent amount, and compounding evidence for that provider.

Exclusion rule

Exclude generic GLP-1 prices that cannot be assigned to one medication, amounts with no stated monthly cadence or monthly equivalent, branded-only prices, providers without a current first-party evidence object, and declared duplicate merchant aliases.

Limits

The index mixes the lowest eligible advertised starting, introductory, bundle-equivalent, oral, injectable, and microdose amounts when those are the provider's lowest explicit monthly figures. It is not an all-in quote and does not normalize dose, supply, membership, labs, shipping, commitment, eligibility, or later charges.

A weekly observation is appended only when the regeneration job actually runs against the then-current tracker and source-file hashes. Earlier weeks are never backfilled, and week-over-week movers remain unavailable until two real observations exist.

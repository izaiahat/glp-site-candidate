What you will find

  • Published price on each result

    The provider's published ongoing price and plan label, the first-month price where it differs, the source link and the capture date.

  • Medication path

    Compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide programs matched to your answers. Brand-name answers go to that medication's comparison page.

  • Terms on the linked review

    Each result links to the program's source-checked review, which covers cancellation and renewal terms where the provider publishes them.

  • Clinician and pharmacy details on the review

    Prescriber network and named compounding pharmacies, as the provider publishes them. Unpublished details are shown as unknown, not assumed.

A publisher, not a clinic

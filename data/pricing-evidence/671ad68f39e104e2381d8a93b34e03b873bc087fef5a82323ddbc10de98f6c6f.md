What are GLP-1's?

GLP-1s work with your body's natural biology to support meaningful, sustainable weight loss. They mimic a hormone your body already produces—helping regulate appetite, stabilize blood sugar, and create a stronger sense of fullness after meals.

At AltrX, we use GLP-1 therapies, create a smarter path to weight management. These treatments are guided by licensed clinicians and tailored to your individual goals.

GLP-1/GIP vs. GLP-1

While both support weight loss, GLP-1/GIP has a dual mechanism — activating both GLP-1 and GIP receptors — compared to the single GLP-1 pathway. GLP-1/GIP users lost an average of 15–22% of body weight versus 12–15% with GLP-1 alone in clinical trials.

GLP-1/GIP may offer enhanced metabolic benefits due to its dual action, but individual responses vary. Your clinician will help determine which medication aligns best with your health profile, goals, and tolerance.

The Science Behind It

Activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors — a dual-hormone approach. GLP-1 helps reduce appetite and slows digestion, while GIP enhances insulin response and may improve fat metabolism. Together, they create synergistic effects on weight loss and metabolic health.

This dual mechanism addresses multiple pathways simultaneously — appetite regulation, blood sugar control, energy expenditure, and fat storage — resulting in more comprehensive metabolic support than single-hormone medications.

How It Feels to Take GLP-1/GIP

Similar to other GLP-1 medications, most users experience significantly reduced appetite and cravings within the first week. Meals become more satisfying with smaller portions, and the constant urge to snack tends to fade naturally.

Initial side effects may include mild nausea, digestive changes, or temporary fatigue — typically resolving within 2–4 weeks. The dual-action mechanism may provide more sustained energy throughout the day. Dosing begins low and increases gradually to optimize comfort and effectiveness.

**Storage:** Refrigerate before first use; can be kept at room temperature for up to 21 days after opening

How does Measured work?

Measured is a comprehensive, personalized approach to weight loss and longevity care. We support your health goals through prescription medications (when appropriate), ongoing clinical care, and nutrition support.

Start by taking a short quiz to confirm eligibility. You'll then complete a medical assessment with more detail about your health history, lifestyle, and goals. If needed, you may also be asked to complete lab work.

Your provider will review your information and create a personalized treatment plan designed to support your weight loss or longevity goals.

Your treatment plan may include:

- Prescription medications, if applicable
- Unlimited messaging with your Care Team — including your provider, Registered Dietitian, and care coordinator
- Personalized meal guides with recipes
- Educational content
- Access to a community of 10,000+ others

Measured is here for you every step of the way with ongoing support, unlimited messaging, and plans that evolve with you.

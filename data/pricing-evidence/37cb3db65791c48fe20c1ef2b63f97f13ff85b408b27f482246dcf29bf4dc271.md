## How Oral Semaglutide Works

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist — it mimics a natural hormone your body already produces after eating.

### Mimics GLP-1

Activates GLP-1 receptors in the brain that regulate appetite and satiety signals.

### Slows gastric emptying

Food moves through your stomach more slowly, so you feel full longer after meals.

### Reduces cravings

Targets reward centers in the brain to lower the urge to overeat or snack.

Variant detail

### Why ondansetron is compounded into the tablet

GLP-1 medications work in part by slowing gastric emptying, which can trigger nausea — especially during the first weeks of treatment. Compounding a low dose of ondansetron, a 5-HT3 receptor antagonist, into the same tablet helps blunt that nausea signal at the source so patients can tolerate titration and stay on therapy long enough to see results.

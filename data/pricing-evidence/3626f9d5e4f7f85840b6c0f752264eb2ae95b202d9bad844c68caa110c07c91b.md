How often do I need to take it?+

Injections are designed for once-weekly dosing, providing steady, structured support for your daily routine as directed by your licensed provider.

Do the injections hurt?+

We use ultra-fine needles designed for subcutaneous use — just beneath the skin — which makes the process virtually painless and simple to perform at home.

How may it support my wellness goals?+

It helps support metabolic health as part of a Nu Image Medical provider-guided plan, making it easier to maintain lifestyle changes consistently over time.

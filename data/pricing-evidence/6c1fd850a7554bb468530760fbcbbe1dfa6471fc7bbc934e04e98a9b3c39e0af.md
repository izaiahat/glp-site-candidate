## Frequently asked questions

Is Ozempic® the same as compounded semaglutide?

The active molecule is the same. Ozempic® is FDA-approved and manufactured by Novo Nordisk. Compounded semaglutide is prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies — typically at a much lower cost.

Will my insurance cover it?

Sometimes — especially if you have type-2 diabetes. Coverage for weight loss alone is harder. We check coverage during your assessment.

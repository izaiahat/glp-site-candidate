## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. It mimics a hormone your body makes naturally to help regulate appetite and blood sugar.\*

How does Semaglutide work?

Semaglutide signals the brain regarding satiety, slows how quickly food leaves the stomach, and supports insulin sensitivity. This may result in reduced hunger and smaller portions when paired with healthy lifestyle changes.\*

What are the benefits of Semaglutide for weight management?

Semaglutide may help reduce appetite and cravings, support more stable blood sugar, and lead to sustainable weight management when combined with nutrition and activity changes, all under physician supervision.\*

How is Semaglutide taken?

The compounded injectable is a once-weekly subcutaneous injection, typically into the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm. The oral option is a buccal lozenge placed between the cheek and gum and allowed to dissolve as directed by your doctor. Your physician will guide you through dose titration over the first weeks either way.

What is the difference between injectable and oral Semaglutide?

Both formats contain Semaglutide. The injectable is a once-weekly shot, while the oral lozenge is a daily dose that dissolves between the cheek and gum, no needles required. Your provider can help you choose based on your preference and treatment plan.

What results can I expect with Semaglutide?

Results vary by individual and depend on your starting point, dose, and lifestyle habits. Your provider will adjust your plan based on how you respond.\*

How long until I see results?

Many patients notice changes in appetite within the first few weeks, with progress occurring over a 3 to 12 month plan when paired with healthy nutrition and movement.\*

How long do I need to stay on Semaglutide?

Treatment length is personalized with your physician and depends on your goals. Many patients continue on a maintenance dose to help sustain results after their initial phase.

Is Semaglutide safe for long-term use?

Semaglutide is intended for medically supervised, ongoing use, with your physician monitoring your progress and adjusting your plan as needed. Share your full medical history so your provider can confirm it's appropriate for long-term use in your case.

Do I need to change my diet while taking Semaglutide?

Semaglutide works alongside healthy nutrition and regular movement; it isn't a replacement for lifestyle changes. Your physician can help you build a plan that fits your routine.

How much does the online visit cost?

The initial online consultation is included with your subscription. You only pay for the medication if a Strut physician determines treatment is appropriate.

What are the common side effects of Semaglutide?

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, abdominal pain, decreased appetite, fatigue, headache, and injection-site reactions.

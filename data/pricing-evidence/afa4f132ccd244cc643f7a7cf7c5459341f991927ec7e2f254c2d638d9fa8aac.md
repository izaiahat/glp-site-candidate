How does this medication help with weight loss?

Tirzepatide activates GLP-1 and GIP receptors to improve insulin sensitivity, reduce appetite, and slow digestion — helping you eat less while feeling full longer.

Do I need to take it daily or weekly?

It’s a once-weekly injection, typically administered in the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm. Our licensed providers guide you through proper dosing and titration.

## The questions most patients ask first.

### Is this the same as Wegovy / Ozempic / Zepbound?

Try Ageless dispenses **compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide** — the same active ingredients found in brand-name Wegovy, Ozempic, Mounjaro and Zepbound — from state-licensed U.S. pharmacies. Your clinician can explain how compounded therapy compares to the brand-name option for your situation.

### Injection or drops — which should I choose?

**Injection** has the most clinical trial data and produces greater average weight loss. **Tirzepatide drops** are needle-free, daily under-the-tongue, and ideal for travel or needle-averse patients. Your clinician will recommend based on your goals, comfort, and budget.

### What about side effects?

Most common: **nausea, mild constipation, fatigue** in the first 2-4 weeks as your body adapts — almost always resolves with proper titration. Less common: vomiting, diarrhoea. Rare but serious: pancreatitis, gallbladder issues. Your clinician monitors and adjusts to minimise side effects throughout.

### Will I gain it all back if I stop?

Some weight regain is common after discontinuation — this is true for every weight-loss intervention. Best practice is to taper gradually under clinician guidance, maintain protein intake, keep moving, and stay on a low maintenance dose long-term if needed. **It’s a tool, not a sprint.**

## How Injectable Semaglutide Works

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist delivered via a once-weekly subcutaneous injection for maximum absorption.

### Activates GLP-1 receptors

Signals fullness to the hypothalamus, naturally reducing hunger between meals.

### Slows digestion

Delays gastric emptying so you stay satisfied longer after eating.

### Lowers caloric intake

Patients naturally eat less without feeling deprived or constantly hungry.

### Steady, lasting results

Weekly dosing maintains consistent blood levels for reliable appetite control.

### Why B-12 is compounded into the injection

GLP-1 therapy lowers appetite and slows gastric emptying, which can reduce dietary intake and absorption of vitamin B-12 — a cofactor essential for red blood cell formation and cellular energy. Co-formulating B-12 into the weekly injection helps maintain healthy levels so patients feel energized while losing weight.

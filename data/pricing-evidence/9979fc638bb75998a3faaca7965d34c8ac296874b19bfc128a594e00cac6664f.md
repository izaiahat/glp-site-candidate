## Your weight loss journey  with OrderlyMeds

Here’s a one year timeline of what to expect when you start a GLP-1 medication with OrderlyMeds.

[**I’m ready to start**](https://patients.orderlymeds.com/eligibility)

#### Month 1

**Initial health screening & starting medication**

Share medical history and weight goals with your provider, start GLP-1 therapy, and monitor how you feel and your progress.

#### Month 2 & 3

**Adjustment & titration period**

Your 3-month starter plan includes monthly dose increases designed to support steady results. If you transferred in, check in with our provider to tailor your medication to your needs.

#### Months 4-6

**Establish lifestyle changes**

Tap into OrderlyMeds lifestyle recommendations to form healthy, sustainable habits that move you toward your weight loss goals.

#### Months 7-9

**Track & refine**

Leverage our mobile app to track progress as you adjust your medication and embrace a healthier lifestyle. Refine your lifestyle strategies, and adjust your weight loss goals as needed.

#### Months 10-12

**Long-term planning**

Focus on sustaining whole-body health and consider next steps for your GLP-1 therapy. Celebrate your year-long improvements and set new wellness goals.

**What is OrderlyMeds?**

OrderlyMeds is a 100% online platform that connects patients to licensed healthcare professionals in all 50 states.

Through OrderlyMeds, you can connect with licensed medical providers who can recommend personalized treatment plans, including prescription GLP-1 medications if appropriate, delivered straight to your door.

**How does OrderlyMeds work?**

**Tell us about yourself** – Share your medical history, health goals, and current medications through our secure online health screening form.

**Get reviewed by a doctor** – A licensed physician checks your info and, if required by your state, chats with you via video to make sure a GLP-1 treatment is right for you.

**Receive your personalized treatment plan** – If prescribed, you’ll receive a notification that your prescription information has been sent your OrderlyMeds portal.

**Medication to your door** – If prescribed, your medication will be compounded for your needs at one of our U.S. based licensed pharmacies, and shipped in discreet packaging with simple instructions directly to your door.

**Do I need insurance?**

Nope! OrderlyMeds doesn’t require insurance. We offer transparent, all inclusive pricing for your care, which covers your online visits with your provider, your medication, direct access to your provider through secure messaging, and over $3,700 in partnership perks and expert-led resources to support a healthy lifestyle change — all for one low price.

**What states is OrderlyMeds available in?**

Our providers are licensed to prescribe medication in all 50 states. Prescriptions are based on patient eligibility, and patient-specific compounding needs.

**Are OrderlyMeds pharmacies regulated?**

Yes, 503A pharmacies are licensed by state boards of pharmacy. OrderlyMeds exclusively works with pharmacies based in the United States, including SmartPharmaRx, Casa, and PerfectRx/PerfectionRx. Our affiliated pharmacies are led by [credentialed pharmacists](https://orderlymeds.com/resources/resources-article/glp1-compounding-pharmacist/), and each batch of medication formulated for you undergoes rigorous testing for sterility and potency before it ever reaches you.

## FrequentlyAskedQuestions

Is YourEra right for me?

YourEra is designed for adults ready to make a meaningful change in their weight, energy, or long-term health with medical guidance. The intake quiz and a licensed clinician determine whether treatment is appropriate. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

How do prescriptions work?

A licensed provider in your state reviews your intake and connects with you. If treatment is clinically appropriate, they issue a prescription filled by a state-licensed U.S. pharmacy and shipped to your door. Prescriptions are never automatic.

What are compounded medications?

Compounded medications are prepared by state-licensed compounding pharmacies to a clinician's specifications. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved, and the FDA does not verify their safety, effectiveness, or quality. That is why every YourEra prescription is physician-directed and sourced only from licensed U.S. pharmacies.

Can I use insurance or HSA/FSA?

Programs are priced for transparent out-of-pocket payment, with no surprise bills or prior authorizations. Many members use HSA/FSA funds; check with your plan administrator for eligibility.

Can I pause or cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. Pause or cancel your program or Optimum membership from your patient portal at any time, and your care team will help you do it safely.

How will my package be delivered?

Your medication ships from a licensed U.S. pharmacy in discreet packaging, with no indication of the contents on the outside. Please note that vial labels shown on this website are for marketing purposes only. The vial you receive will have more discreet labeling.

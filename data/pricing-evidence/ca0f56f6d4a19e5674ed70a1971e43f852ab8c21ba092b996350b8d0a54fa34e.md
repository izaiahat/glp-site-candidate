How does tirzepatide work?

Tirzepatide works by activating both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, hormones involved in appetite regulation, blood sugar control, and digestion. It may help reduce hunger, support portion control, and assist with weight management when combined with clinician supervision and lifestyle modifications.

What are common tirzepatide side effects?

Common tirzepatide side effects may include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, stomach discomfort, reduced appetite, and fatigue. Side effects vary by individual and patients should consult a licensed healthcare provider before starting treatment.

How does semaglutide work?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist that helps regulate appetite, blood sugar, and digestion. It may help patients feel fuller longer, reduce hunger, and support weight management when combined with lifestyle changes and clinician supervision.

What are common semaglutide side effects?

Common side effects may include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, stomach discomfort, reduced appetite, and fatigue. Side effects vary by individual and patients should consult a licensed healthcare provider before starting treatment.

What is the difference between tirzepatide and semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist, while tirzepatide works as both a GLP-1 and GIP receptor agonist. Both medications may support weight management and metabolic health, but treatment suitability depends on individual medical evaluation.

How much weight can I lose with GLP-1 medications?

Weight loss results vary depending on dosage, consistency, nutrition, activity level, and individual response. Many patients may experience meaningful weight reduction over time when treatment is combined with lifestyle modifications and clinician supervision.

Who may qualify for semaglutide or tirzepatide treatment?

Eligibility is determined by a licensed healthcare provider based on factors such as medical history, current health status, BMI, and treatment goals. Patients complete an online intake and clinical review before treatment consideration.

How long does it take to see results?

Some patients may begin noticing appetite changes and early weight loss within the first few weeks, while more significant results often develop over several months of consistent treatment and lifestyle support.

Are GLP-1 medications safe?

GLP-1 medications may be appropriate for many patients when prescribed and monitored by licensed healthcare professionals. Treatment suitability and risks should always be discussed with a qualified provider based on individual medical history.

What is a 503A pharmacy?

A 503A pharmacy is a state-licensed compounding pharmacy that prepares customized medications for individual patients based on a valid prescription from a licensed healthcare provider.

Is compounded GLP-1 FDA approved?

Compounded medications are not FDA approved in the same way commercially manufactured drugs are approved. Compounded GLP-1 medications may be prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies pursuant to a valid prescription from a licensed healthcare provider.

How are compounded GLP-1 medications made?

Compounded GLP-1 medications are prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies that follow strict quality standards. They use pharmaceutical-grade ingredients to create customized formulations based on a healthcare provider's prescription.

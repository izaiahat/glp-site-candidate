## What is Semaglutide Sublingual?

Semaglutide Sublingual delivers the same GLP-1 receptor agonist used in injectable semaglutide, but through drops placed under the tongue. This needle-free alternative absorbs through the oral mucosa, making it ideal for those who prefer to avoid injections.

Daily dosing maintains consistent medication levels throughout the day. Each prescription includes guidance on proper administration technique.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How does sublingual compare to injectable semaglutide?

Both contain the same active ingredient. Injectable typically has higher bioavailability, but sublingual offers the convenience of no needles. Your provider can help determine which is best for your needs and preferences.

How do I take sublingual semaglutide?

Place the prescribed number of drops under your tongue and hold for 30-60 seconds before swallowing. Take on an empty stomach, at least 30 minutes before eating or drinking.

Will I have the same side effects as injectable?

Side effects are similar to injectable semaglutide, primarily gastrointestinal symptoms like nausea. Starting with a low dose helps minimize these effects.

Can I switch to injectable later?

Yes, many patients start with sublingual and switch to injectable, or vice versa. Your provider can help you transition between formulations.

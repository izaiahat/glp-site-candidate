## What is Semaglutide Injectable?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist that helps regulate appetite and food intake. Originally developed for type 2 diabetes, clinical trials demonstrated significant weight loss benefits, leading to FDA approval for chronic weight management.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can I expect to lose?

Clinical trials show average weight loss of 15-20% of body weight over 68 weeks. Individual results vary based on adherence, diet, exercise, and starting weight. Most patients see noticeable results within 8-12 weeks.

What are the common side effects?

The most common side effects are gastrointestinal: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation. These typically improve as your body adjusts. Starting with a low dose and gradually increasing helps minimize side effects.

How long do I need to take semaglutide?

Semaglutide is typically used as long as weight management benefits continue. Discontinuing may result in weight regain. Your provider will work with you on a long-term plan.

Can I take semaglutide with other medications?

Semaglutide may interact with certain medications, especially those affecting blood sugar. Provide a complete medication list during intake so your provider can assess safety.

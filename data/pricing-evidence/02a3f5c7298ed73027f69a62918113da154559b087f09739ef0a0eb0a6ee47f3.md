## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Oral Semaglutide?

Oral Semaglutide is a compounded buccal lozenge containing Semaglutide, a GLP-1 receptor agonist used for medically supervised weight management. It dissolves between the cheek and gum, so no injections are required.

How do I take Oral Semaglutide lozenges?

Place the lozenge between your cheek and gum and allow it to dissolve fully, as directed by your doctor. Most patients take one lozenge per day at the same time.

How does Semaglutide reduce appetite?

Semaglutide mimics the GLP-1 hormone your body makes naturally. It signals the brain regarding satiety, slows how quickly food leaves the stomach, and may help increase insulin release, together helping reduce hunger throughout the day.\*

What results can I expect?

Results vary by individual and depend on your starting point, dose, and lifestyle habits. Many patients see progress over a 3 to 12 month plan when Semaglutide is paired with healthy nutrition and movement.\*

How does Oral Semaglutide compare to the injectable?

Both formats contain Semaglutide and are prescribed for the same purpose. The oral lozenge dissolves between the cheek and gum once daily with no needles, while the injectable is a once-weekly subcutaneous shot. Injectable Semaglutide may offer slightly higher bioavailability, but the lozenge is a needle-free alternative for patients who want to avoid injections.

How long do I need to stay on Semaglutide?

Treatment length is personalized with your physician and depends on your goals. Many patients continue on a maintenance dose to help sustain results after their initial phase.

Is Semaglutide safe for long-term use?

Semaglutide is intended for medically supervised, ongoing use, with your physician monitoring your progress and adjusting your plan as needed. Share your full medical history so your provider can confirm it's appropriate for long-term use in your case.

Do I need to change my diet?

Semaglutide works alongside healthy nutrition and regular movement; it isn't a replacement for lifestyle changes. Your physician can help you build a plan that fits your routine.

How long until I see results?

Many patients notice appetite changes within the first weeks, with progress occurring over 3 to 12 months when paired with healthy nutrition and movement.\*

How much does the online visit cost?

The initial online consultation is included with your subscription. You only pay for the medication if a Strut physician determines treatment is appropriate.

Is shipping free?

Yes. Your monthly price includes free shipping along with the medication and physician consultations, with no hidden fees.

Is medical necessity required for compounded Semaglutide?

Yes. Compounded Oral Semaglutide is intended for use only when a clinical need cannot be met by an FDA-approved product. A Strut physician reviews your information to confirm compounded treatment is appropriate before it's prescribed.

## What is Tirzepatide?

Tirzepatide is a first-in-class medication that activates both GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide) and GLP-1 receptors. This dual-action approach provides enhanced appetite regulation and metabolic benefits compared to GLP-1-only medications.

Clinical trials have shown tirzepatide to produce significant weight loss results. Our medical team provides ongoing supervision and dose titration to optimize your outcomes while managing side effects.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How is tirzepatide different from semaglutide?

Tirzepatide activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors, while semaglutide only activates GLP-1. This dual action may provide enhanced weight loss and metabolic benefits. Clinical trials show tirzepatide produces greater average weight loss.

What weight loss can I expect with tirzepatide?

Clinical trials showed average weight loss of 20-25% of body weight at the highest doses. Individual results vary based on dosing, adherence, diet, and exercise habits.

Are the side effects stronger than semaglutide?

Side effects are similar in type (primarily gastrointestinal) but may be slightly more common with tirzepatide. Gradual dose titration helps minimize these effects. Most side effects improve over time.

Should I start with tirzepatide or semaglutide?

This depends on your goals, medical history, and preferences. Some patients start with semaglutide and switch to tirzepatide if they want enhanced results. Your provider can help determine the best starting point.

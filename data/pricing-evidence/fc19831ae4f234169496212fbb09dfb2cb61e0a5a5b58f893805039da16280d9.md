## Cost and pricing

How much does it cost?

Our most popular GLP-1/GIP injectable product is available for as little as $149 per month when purchased as a 2 month starter program ($299 total for 2 months), or when purchased month-to-month.

Other GLP-1 injections are available for as little as $74 per month when purchased as a 2 month starter program ($149 total for 2 months), or when purchased month to month.

The price will be the same regardless if purchased as a 2 month starter pack or month-to-month.

We also offer brand name medications, with our month-to-month Wegovy®\* priced at $1,839 per month, and our month-to-month Zepbound®\*\* priced at $1,498 per month.

Please visit our [Plans & pricing](https://orderlymedsdev.wpenginepowered.com/pricing/) page to see all available plan options and what they cost.

Our pricing covers in inclusive of everything: your doctor’s visit, medication, syringes, and shipping. We also provide comprehensive onboarding webinars, videos, and additional support material, as well as access to free and discounted partnership perks.

Are there any membership fees? What are the hidden costs?

We have no membership fees or hidden costs—what you see is what you pay!

Do you accept CareCredit® cards?

No, we do not accept CareCredit® cards currently.

Does the price increase with the dose?

No! All of our medication is available for the same price per dosage.

Can I use my HSA/FSA card?

Yes, we do accept both Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Cards (FSA) as forms of payment. Our medications and services are typically considered eligible expenses for these funds. You can use these cards just like a regular debit card for payment. If needed, we can provide documentation for your records. This option offers flexibility in managing your healthcare expenses. Please ensure your card has sufficient funds to cover the cost of the medication and be aware that some administrators may require expense verification which you may download from your customer portal. If you encounter any issues with your card or need additional information, feel free to send us an email at: **[support@orderlymeds.com](mailto:support@orderlymeds.com)**.

Do you accept insurance?

No, we do not accept insurance. However, you can use your HSA or FSA card for payment.

Do you offer payment plans?

Currently, we require full payment for each month’s medication at the time of ordering. Our service is offered on a month-to-month basis, so you only commit to one month at a time and aren’t locked into a long-term financial contract. While we don’t have payment plans at the moment, we regularly review our payment options based on customer feedback for potential future changes.

Can I purchase multiple vials at a time?

Yes, you can purchase multiple vials at a time. Please visit our [Plans & pricing](https://orderlymeds.com/pricing/) page to see all available multi-month plan options.

Do you accept payments from mobile wallets like Apple Pay, Google Pay or Venmo?

We currently only accept credit card and FSA/HSA payments. While we don’t offer mobile wallet payment options at the moment, we regularly review our payment options based on customer feedback for potential future changes.

We request payment during your medical screening to streamline the process, similar to how you pay a copay before seeing a doctor. At OrderlyMeds, your satisfaction is our priority, and we aim to provide clear and reliable service throughout your experience.

Do I need to pay extra for the syringes and alcohol prep pads?

Our pharmacies provide all the syringes and alcohol prep pads necessary for your injections, no need to pay extra.

Do you price match?

While we do not offer price matching, we work hard to keep our medications as affordable as possible. This allows us to maintain consistent pricing across all doses, ensuring you receive the best value for your treatment.

## Orders and shipping

How long does it take to ship?

Your prescription will be approved within 24-48 hours, and the pharmacy will process and ship it within 2-3 days. We will email you the tracking information as soon as it becomes available. You can expect to receive your medication within 8-10 business days of signing up, with tracking details provided once your order has shipped. We recommend downloading the UPS or FedEx Delivery Manager app to get real-time updates on your order once the pharmacy generates the shipping label.

Can you expedite my order?

No, we cannot expedite orders at this time.

What is OrderlyMed’s refund policy?

When you sign up, you can cancel anytime before the doctor finalizes your prescription. Once your prescription is written after your medical consultation, cancellation is no longer possible. If you’re denied a prescription, don’t worry—we’ll fully refund your payment!

How long does the refund process take?

Refunds may take 3-5 business days to appear in your bank account, depending on your bank’s processing times. Unfortunately, this is something beyond our control at OrderlyMeds. Thank you for your understanding!

Can I cancel my OrderlyMeds order?

When you sign up, you can cancel any time before the doctor finalizes your prescription, unless you’ve selected a multi-month plan. Our standard sign-up process includes month-to-month plans as well as multi-month options. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out!

Can you ship multiple months of medication?

You have options for both of our GLP-1 treatments for multi-month ordering. Please review the current [Plans & pricing](https://orderlymeds.com/pricing/) for the most up to date options.

Can you send me replacement syringes if ever I accidentally lose or damage the ones that I have?

If you need extra syringes or accidentally damage the ones from the pharmacy, we recommend visiting your local pharmacy or ordering more online.

How do I make a claim for lost packages?

Please report a lost or stolen package through the UPS or FedEx portal, and they’ll provide you with a claim ID. Once you have the claim ID, just send it to us, and we can pass it along to the pharmacy to verify and arrange for a replacement.

How long until I receive my order?

We strive to be as transparent as possible throughout the entire process, including before payment. You can expect your shipment to arrive in about 8-10 business days (though we often do a little better than that)!

Can I ask for medication refill earlier than the check-in date?

You can check in earlier or later than your scheduled date! Our system will send you a reminder 15 days before your actual check-in date.

How do I track my prescription once it has left the pharmacy?

We will provide tracking details once the pharmacy shares them with us. For real-time updates, we recommend downloading the UPS or FedEx Delivery Manager app to receive notifications as soon as the pharmacy generates the shipping label.

What if my medication got warm during shipping?

Worried your medication got warm during shipping? Don’t be.

A temperature excursion study conducted by our pharmacy partners confirmed that your medication remains fully potent for at least 72 hours (3 days) during shipping, even without cold packs or refrigeration. It was tested at temperatures as high as 104°F (40°C), including common spikes that can occur in transit.

While the medication is stable at typical room temperatures, please refrigerate it when you arrive to ensure long-term effectiveness.

What is included in your program?

Our pricing is inclusive of everything: your doctor’s visit, medication, syringes, and shipping. We also provide comprehensive onboarding webinars, videos, and additional support materials, as well as access to free and discounted partnership perks.

[Click here](https://patients.orderlymeds.com/eligibility) to get started and check your eligibility. Who is eligible for compounded GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP medications through OrderlyMeds?

Our licensed providers will determine your eligibility for a compounded GLP-1 injection prescription. Our general requirements include:

– You must be 18 years old or older

– Have a BMI greater than 20 if you are already taking a GLP-1 medication, or greater than 25 if you are new to GLP-1 medications

– Are not pregnant

– Do not have certain medical conditions (i.e. thyroid cancer or hyperthyroidism, gastroparesis, pancreatic cancer or pancreatitis, MEN2 syndrome, or inflammatory bowel disease).

– Require a compounded preparation that produces a significant difference for your needs compared to the FDA-approved product

This is not a complete list of qualifying requirements. As a part of our process, you will need to complete an online health screening with our telehealth providers to ultimately determine whether you are eligible to be prescribed compounded GLP-1 injections.

How do refills work?

You will receive text and email reminders before your check-in, where you can share how you’re feeling on the medication and decide if you want to stay at your current dose or request an increase. The medical team will review your feedback, write a new prescription, and send it to the pharmacy. After that, the shipping process will be the same as before, and you’ll receive tracking details via text once it ships.

When will I hear from the doctor?

Provided there are no delays, within 24-48 hours after completing your health screen, your provider will send you a message through the patient portal chat. We’ll alert you via text when you get new messages. This timeframe begins once you have successfully submitted all required information. To ensure the fastest possible response, please complete all sections of the health screen thoroughly, respond promptly if additional information is requested, and double-check that all submitted information is accurate. If you experience any issues or do not hear from the doctor within the specified timeframe, please contact our customer support team. If you live in Louisiana, Mississippi, New Mexico, Rhode Island, Kansas or West Virginia, you will be required by state law to perform a video consult with our medical team.

Are lab/bloodwork needed to start with OrderlyMeds?

OrderlyMeds does not require bloodwork for prescribing our medications. Our healthcare providers make prescribing decisions based on the medical information you provide during the consultation, including your health history, current medications, and any relevant symptoms or conditions. However, while bloodwork is not required, our providers might recommend it in certain cases if they believe it would benefit your overall health management. Regular check-ups with your primary care physician, which may include bloodwork, are always encouraged as part of maintaining your general health. If you have recent bloodwork results that you think might be relevant, you are welcome to share them, but it is not a requirement for our service.

What is a GLP-1 medication? How does it work?

Our medications belong to a class of medications known as glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists. It mimics the GLP-1 hormone that is released in the gastrointestinal tract in response to eating. One role of GLP-1 is to prompt the body to produce more insulin, which reduces blood glucose (sugar). GLP-1 in higher amounts also interacts with the parts of the brain that [reduce appetite](https://orderlymeds.com/resources/how-long-does-it-take-for-semaglutide-to-suppress-appetite/) and signal a feeling of fullness.

What are compounded GLP-1 medications?

Drug compounding involves mixing or altering ingredients to create medications tailored to individual patients’ needs. The FDA allows compounding pharmacies to fulfill medications for patients that cannot be treated with an FDA-approved medication, such as a patient who has an allergy to a certain dye and needs a medication to be made without it, or an elderly patient or a child who cannot swallow a tablet or capsule and needs a medicine in a liquid dosage form.

Compounded GLP-1 and GLP-1/GIP are not FDA approved and do not undergo safety, effectiveness, or manufacturing review and may only be obtained with a prescription from a licensed health care provider.3

With OrderlyMeds, you can trust that our compounded GLP-1 medication is made from its base form, not the salt form. Our compounding pharmacies adhere to USP standards and federal guidelines, and we thoroughly vet each pharmacy for accreditations, licenses, and manufacturing processes.”

Why do some patients need compounded drugs?

A drug may be compounded for a patient who cannot be treated with an FDA-approved medication, such as a patient who has an allergy to a certain dye and needs a medication to be made without it, or an elderly patient or a child who cannot swallow a tablet or capsule and needs a medicine in a liquid dosage form. Practitioners in hospitals, clinics, and other health care facilities sometimes provide compounded drugs to patients when an FDA-approved drug is not medically appropriate to treat them. In these situations, compounding can serve an important patient need.

How much weight can I lose on a GLP-1 medication?

Weight loss varies by individual, and no medication can guarantee specific results.

In clinical trials of FDA-approved medications used alongside diet and exercise:

- Participants taking Wegovy® (semaglutide 2.4 mg) lost an average of about 15% of body weight over 68 weeks.¹
- Participants taking Zepbound® (tirzepatide 15 mg) lost an average of about 20% of body weight over 72 weeks.²

These are average results observed under clinical trial conditions. Individual outcomes depend on many factors, including medical history, dose, and adherence to lifestyle changes. Your provider will determine whether treatment is appropriate for you.

Any clinical trial results referenced on this website apply solely to FDA-approved branded medications. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and do not have equivalent clinical trial data establishing the same safety, efficacy, or outcomes.

Weight loss effects with taking GLP-1 medications are unique to each person. Weight loss varies by individual, and no medication can guarantee specific results. A safe and sustainable weight loss target is 1 – 2lbs per week. Factors like starting weight, diet, exercise, and metabolism all play a role, and results may vary. Combining GLP-1 medications with healthy eating and regular exercise may offer the most sustainable long term results.

How do I have a consultation with one of your medical providers?

[Click here](https://patients.orderlymeds.com/eligibility) to get started and check your eligibility. If you are eligible for a GLP-1 medication, you’ll select your product and plan option, fill out the health screening form online\*, and pay for your order. Our internal team reviews your information to ensure we have everything we need, and submits you to our provider group to be prescribed. They will review your information and write your prescription before sending it to one of our pharmacies for compounding. If for any reason you are not approved for the medication, we will immediately process a full refund.

\*If you live in Louisiana, Mississippi, New Mexico, Rhode Island, Kansas or West Virginia, you will be required by state law to perform a video consult with our medical team.

Who are the OrderlyMeds medical providers? Are they licensed?

OrderlyMeds has licensed providers in all states. Our healthcare professions are board-certified physicians that provide the highest standard of care for your weight loss needs. Our physician network is LegitScript certified, with veteran clinical oversight, available in all 50 states.

How quickly is treatment prescribed?

Creating your account and filling our your online health screening application can be completed in minutes at your convenience. Within 48 hours our licensed medical providers will review your information and prescribe treatment if appropriate. Some states require by law that visits are completed via video, which you can schedule at your convenience. Most patients can schedule their visit within 1-2 days.

Do you have licensed providers in my state?

Yes – our physician network is licensed to prescribe in all 50 states.

Can I talk to a doctor via phone call or video?

Your provider consultation is done by chatting with the doctor via chat in your OrderlyMeds patient portal. If you are located in Louisiana, Mississippi, New Mexico, Kansas or West Virginia, you will consult with your provider via live video. All follow ups with a provider will be conducted via your patient portal chat. We’ll alert you via text any time you have a new message from our medical team.

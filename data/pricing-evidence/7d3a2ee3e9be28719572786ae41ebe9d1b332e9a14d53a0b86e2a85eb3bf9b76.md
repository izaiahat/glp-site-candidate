How is this different from Semaglutide?+

Tirzepatide is a different prescription medication that targets two receptor pathways — GLP-1 and GIP — offering a distinct pharmacological approach. Your Nu Image Medical provider will help determine which option aligns best with your health history and goals.

How often do I take the injections?+

The injections are taken once a week, offering a structured routine guided by the Nu Image Medical team designed to support your wellness goals consistently.

Is this the right option for me?+

This format may be a good fit if you prefer a structured, once-weekly routine. Your licensed provider will review your full health profile during your consultation to help you decide.

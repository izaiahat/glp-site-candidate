## Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a prescription for peptide treatment?

Yes, all peptide treatments require a valid prescription from a licensed healthcare provider. Our medical team reviews your health history and determines if treatment is appropriate for you.

How long does it take to receive my medication?

After your prescription is approved, medications typically ship within 24-48 hours. Most patients receive their order within 3-5 business days via priority shipping.

Are these medications safe?

Our medications are sourced from FDA-registered pharmacies and undergo rigorous quality testing. All treatments are prescribed under clinician supervision with ongoing monitoring.

What if I'm not eligible for treatment?

If our medical team determines that treatment isn't right for you, you'll receive a full refund of any consultation fees. We prioritize patient safety above all else.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time through your patient portal or by contacting our support team. There are no long-term commitments or cancellation fees.

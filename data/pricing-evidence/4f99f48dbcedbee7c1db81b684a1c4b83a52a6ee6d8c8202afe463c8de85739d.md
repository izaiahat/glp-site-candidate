## How billing and renewals work

The price you see at checkout is the price billed. Plans renew at the end of their term, and every renewal carries a standing $25 subscriber credit off the plan price, which is the Renews at column above. You can cancel online in one click at any time, and any unshipped dose is refunded.

## How HealthRX.com compares with brand-name cash prices

At $99/mo, compounded semaglutide works out to about$3.30 per day, against roughly $45 per day for brand-name Wegovy at its $1,349 monthly cash price.

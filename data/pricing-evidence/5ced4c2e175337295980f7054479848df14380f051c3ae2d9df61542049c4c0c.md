FAQ

### Frequently  asked questions

What is the Coivas Challenge?

The Coivas Challenge is a 3-month, risk-free way to try clinician-guided GLP-1 weight loss care. You get a licensed provider evaluation, ongoing support, and medication shipped to your door. If you make no measurable progress after 3 months, we'll refund your Challenge payments in full.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

If you remain continuously enrolled for your full 3-month subscription, follow your clinician's dosing and refill instructions, and your verified final weight is equal to or greater than your verified starting weight, you may be eligible for a full refund of subscription fees paid during that period. See our Terms for complete eligibility requirements.

What medications are included?

The Challenge covers compounded semaglutide or compounded tirzepatide — both once-weekly GLP-1 injections prescribed by a licensed clinician based on your health history and goals. Brand-name medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound are not included.

Who is eligible for the Challenge?

The Challenge is available to new Coivas customers enrolling for the first time. A licensed clinician will review your intake to determine whether GLP-1 treatment is appropriate for you. Participation does not guarantee that medication will be prescribed.

How do I verify my starting weight?

You must submit or complete a valid starting-weight verification within seven days of enrollment, unless Coivas confirms your starting weight has been otherwise verified. Accurate weight reporting is required for guarantee eligibility.

Can I cancel during the Challenge?

You can pause or cancel your plan at any time from your member portal. However, any cancellation, pause, lapse, or gap in enrollment during the 3-month period will void the money-back guarantee.

What counts as progress?

Progress is measured by comparing your verified final weight to your verified starting weight after completing the full 3-month subscription period. If your final weight is lower than your starting weight, you have made measurable progress.

Does insurance cover the Challenge?

Coivas does not require insurance — the Challenge operates on a simple self-pay model with transparent, all-inclusive pricing. HSA and FSA reimbursement may be available depending on your plan.

How much does it cost?

From $99/month on a 12-month prepaid semaglutide plan ($1,163 today; $1,188 at renewal). Tirzepatide plans start at $179/month. You confirm the exact plan before payment.

Which medication will I get?

That is the clinician's decision, not a menu choice. Your assessment answers and health history point toward semaglutide or tirzepatide, and the provider reviewing your file confirms it — or tells you neither is right for you.

Do I need insurance?

No. HealthRX.com checkout is cash-pay and uses transparent plan pricing.

Is it a big needle?

No. It is a once-weekly injection with a very thin, short needle into the soft part of the stomach or thigh. Most people say it is far easier than they expected, and your provider walks you through exactly how before you start.

Is it safe?

GLP-1 medications are prescription treatments, which is why a licensed provider reviews your full health history first and starts you at a low dose that increases slowly. They are not right for everyone, and your provider will tell you if it is not a fit for you.

How much weight could I lose?

In large published trials, people lost meaningful weight over many months, not overnight. Individual results vary by person, dose, and adherence, and no specific outcome is guaranteed.

Will I gain the weight back?

Weight management is ongoing. Many people continue on a maintenance plan, and your provider helps you find the right long-term approach for you.

What happens after I pay?

A licensed clinician reviews your order and health answers. Once approved, your medication ships directly to your door with free shipping, and our team sends order updates by email.

What if I am not approved?

Treatment does not proceed and no prescription ships unless a licensed provider determines that it is medically appropriate. The clinician or support team will follow up with next steps.

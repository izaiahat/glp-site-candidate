## Frequently asked questions about our GLP-1 starter program

**What’s included in the 2 month starter plan?**

For our compounded GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP injections, you’ll receive all weekly doses of your medications in one shipment and your dosage will be based on the prescription information you provide, your selections during our health screening process, and your provider’s discretion. Your order will also include alcohol pads and the syringes you need to take your injections.

**Are there any membership fees? What are the hidden costs?**

We have no membership fees or hidden costs—what you see is what you pay!

**Does the price increase with the dose?**

No! All of our medication is available for the same price per dosage.

**What price will I pay for my medications after I finish the SimpleStart program?**

Your next month of medication will be priced at our standard monthly rates. Our compounded GLP-1 injections are $149 per month, and out compounded GLP-1/GIP injections are $299 per month. View our [plans & pricing](https://orderlymeds.com/pricing/) page for more detailed plan and pricing options.

**Can I use my HSA/FSA card?**

Yes, we do accept both Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Cards (FSA) as forms of payment. Our medications and services are typically considered eligible expenses for these funds. You can use these cards just like a regular debit card for payment. If needed, we can provide documentation for your records. This option offers flexibility in managing your healthcare expenses. Please ensure your card has sufficient funds to cover the cost of the medication and be aware that some administrators may require expense verification which you may download from your customer portal. If you encounter any issues with your card or need additional information, feel free to send us an email at: **[support@orderlymeds.com](mailto:support@orderlymeds.com)**.

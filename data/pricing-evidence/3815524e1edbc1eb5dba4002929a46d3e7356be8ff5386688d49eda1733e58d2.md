### Why B-12 is compounded into the injection

Tirzepatide lowers appetite and slows gastric emptying, which can reduce dietary intake and absorption of vitamin B-12 — a cofactor essential for red blood cell formation and cellular energy. Co-formulating B-12 into the weekly injection helps maintain healthy levels so patients feel energized while losing weight.

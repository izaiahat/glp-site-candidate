What compounded semaglutide is—and is not

Compounded medicines are prepared for needs an FDA-approved medication cannot meet; FDA gives examples such as making a medicine without a dye a patient is allergic to, or changing a tablet to a liquid for someone unable to swallow it.

FDA compounding questions and answers, checked 9 Sept 2026

This page uses compounded semaglutide to mean the compounded-product category, not a brand-name drug comparison or a claim of FDA-approved equivalence.

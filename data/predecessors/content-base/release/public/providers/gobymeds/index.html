At a glance

Unknown is kept separate from a verified negative.

Medication paths

Compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide, dispensed by 503A or 503B pharmacy partners licensed in the patient's own state, plus the FDA-approved branded pens Zepbound ($1,399/month) and Wegovy ($1,695/month). Prescription and clinician approval required

Insurance

Cash-pay: the terms state GobyMeds does not bill health insurance. HSA and FSA cards are accepted, and no consultation or membership fee is charged on top of the medication price

Consultation or membership

No separate consultation fee and no membership fee up front — the consultation is stated as included in the bundle price. A $50 consultation/clinical-review fee (or another amount disclosed at checkout) may be charged or retained from a refund only if the patient cancels after clinical review has begun.

View consultation or membership source · current as of 2026-08-06

Shipping or timing

UNKNOWN guaranteed end-to-end delivery time. The provider's terms state that shipping and fulfillment estimates are not guaranteed and list clinical, pharmacy, availability, carrier and other possible delays.

Cancellation

Cancellation is stage-dependent, requested through customer support, and effective only once GobyMeds (or the pharmacy/provider whose action is needed) confirms it. Before clinical review begins — and before any prescription, dispensing or shipment — the order is cancelled with a full refund. If a provider declines to prescribe, the order is fully refunded and no consultation fee is retained. If the patient cancels after clinical review has begun, GobyMeds may retain a $50 consultation/clinical-review fee. There is no subscription to cancel: billing is a single payment per order with no auto-renewal.

View cancellation source · current as of 2026-08-06

State evidence

Marketed as provider-reviewed care across all 50 U.S. states. The Terms of Service condition access on the participating provider being licensed where the patient is physically located at the time of care, and state that a residence, mailing, billing or shipping address does not by itself establish eligibility.

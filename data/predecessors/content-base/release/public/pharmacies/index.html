How to read the directory

503 pathway labels describe oversight, not quality.

A 503A pharmacy compounds individual prescriptions under state-board oversight; a 503B outsourcing facility produces larger batches under direct FDA registration. Filters use only source-backed fields. Cards never reserve rows for facts outside the evidence-qualified card contract.

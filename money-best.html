Frequently asked questions

What does best mean in this comparison?
Best means the highest score under the published commercial-evidence rubric. It does not mean best clinical outcome or best medication for you.
Are compounded GLP-1 drugs FDA-approved?
No. FDA says compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and are not reviewed for safety, effectiveness, or quality before marketing.
Does insurance support guarantee coverage?
No. A benefits check or prior-authorization service can help navigate a plan but cannot guarantee formulary coverage, approval, or final cost.
Why are public reviews omitted from scoring?
We did not verify one comparable independent review dataset across all four programs, so public reviews are omitted rather than displayed as artificial zeroes.
Are the provider links active?
Approved commercial partners have active official-site affiliate links. Other programs do not have public partner links. Affiliate relationships do not affect the published evidence scores.

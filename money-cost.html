How much do online GLP-1 programs cost in 2026?

Compare advertised starting prices, annual commitments, medication inclusions, shipping terms, and insurance support across six online GLP-1 programs.

A monthly headline price is not necessarily a monthly total. The amount can change with medication type, dose, membership, labs, shipping, insurance approval, or a multi-month commitment. This page compares what six providers published on July 23, 2026 and marks unstated terms instead of estimating them.

yourEra is our cost-transparency winner because it publishes separate $99 and $169 starting prices, includes shipping, and says there is no long-term contract. Trimi also starts at $99, but its lowest displayed rate is tied to an annual plan. Gala's $179 figure is likewise described with a yearly subscription.

The comparison is commercial information only. A licensed clinician must determine medical eligibility, and compounded drugs are not FDA-approved.

Frequently asked questions

Is $99 the total monthly cost?
Not necessarily. Confirm medication, dose, membership, labs, shipping, taxes, and commitment length at checkout.
Why is Found not the cost winner if it also advertises $99?
Found's page indicates that membership and medication costs may be separate, so its total is less directly comparable to an all-in self-pay headline.
Do lower prices mean lower clinical quality?
This comparison does not measure clinical quality. It scores transparency of published commercial terms.
Can I use HSA or FSA funds?
Some providers state eligibility, but the account administrator and applicable rules determine reimbursement. Verify before purchase.
Are prices guaranteed?
No. They are dated public snapshots, not quotes, and can change by medication, dose, plan, state, and provider.
